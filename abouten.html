The Idea

Our idea is to represent the disparity between human artworks and AI generations when it comes to art, and display reasons to generate content with one's own skill.

To do this, we have compiled generations along certain prompts by various LLMs, as well as enlisting the aid of our team and our friends to create a human equivalent, while also listing what one would consider the benefits of the process, like stress-relief or learning some new technique.

The project seeks to highlight the fact that art and creativity is something so human that it should be left to humanity to create.

It also hopes to display how an LLM will never reach or surpass the limits of human creativity, mostly due to the fact that its own creativity is only the net worth of whatever is most common.

We strive to ensure that all are aware of the working principles behind AI, the limits and responsible usage of AI, and the repercussions of using AI.
